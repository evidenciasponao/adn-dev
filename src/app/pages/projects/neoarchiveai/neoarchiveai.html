THE PROJECT

Why NeoArchiveAI?

Organizations handle contracts, supplier records, customer information and administrative documents that may require strict control over where that information is stored and who can access it.

NeoArchiveAI was designed as a private document management platform that can operate entirely inside an institution's local network, reducing dependency on external cloud services while maintaining a modern web-based experience.
